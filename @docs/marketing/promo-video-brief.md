## 3. Full product description (for script generation)

Polyglot Monster is the translator that explains. It doesn't just give you a word — it tells you what it means, how people really say it, when it's formal or slang, and how to use it in a sentence.

It's built for people who live between languages: emigrants learning the language of a new country while keeping up a second one, polyglots studying several languages in parallel, and professionals whose work, family, or relocation demands more than one tongue. It's the only language-learning tool designed around learning 2+ languages simultaneously — up to four at a time, across 11 languages: English, Russian, Czech, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Ukrainian, Polish, and Kazakh.

There's nothing to install. Polyglot Monster lives inside Telegram, the app you already open every day. Setup takes 30 seconds: pick your native language, pick what you're learning, and send your first phrase.

How it works:

1. **Send a phrase.** A word, an idiom, a whole sentence — even a tricky one like "schadenfreude."
2. **Get rich cards.** One card per language, with CEFR level, register (formal / neutral / slang), transcription, synonyms, idiomatic equivalents, contextual examples, and warnings when a word carries a connotation you didn't expect.
3. **Save and review.** One tap saves it to your personal dictionary. Spaced-repetition review brings it back exactly when you're about to forget it.

Beyond translation:

- **Words from YouTube.** Paste a video link and the bot pulls the useful vocabulary from the transcript into study cards.
- **Pick words by theme.** Choose an angle — travel, work, relocation life, exam prep — and get the words the textbooks skip.
- **AI mentor.** Ask about grammar, phrasing, or usage and get answers tuned to your level in each language.
- **Grammar breakdown and etymology** on any card, with one button.
- **Pronunciation and voice input.** Hear it spoken; send a voice message instead of typing.
- **Daily Word reminders.** Pick your times and timezone; let the bot send words from your dictionary, AI suggestions, or sentences built around a goal you describe — like "preparing for a job interview."
- **Progress that praises real memory, not streaks.** See how many words are in long-term memory, how many are due, and how far you've come — with no guilt and no leaderboards.

Free to start. Plus ($5/mo) unlocks unlimited translations, grammar, etymology, clarifications, and the AI mentor. Pro ($10/mo) adds unlimited video vocabulary, pronunciation, and voice input.

**Open Telegram and try your first card. Your first set of learning cards is one message away.**

## 6. Tone and style guide

- **Voice:** warm, plain-spoken, second person. Short declarative sentences with a two-beat rhythm ("Learn a phrase once. Remember it everywhere."). No exclamation marks, no hype words, no jargon.
- **Show, don't claim:** use real example phrases on screen — "spill the tea", "schadenfreude", "break a leg" — rather than abstract feature lists.
- **Positioning:** a companion / buddy, not a dictionary and not a gamified app. No streaks, no fire icons, no leaderboards, no confetti.
- **Visual identity:** a friendly illustrated monster mascot sorting language cards — playful, not childish. Cozy, earthy palette: cream/paper background, dark ink text, teal accent (#2E9E86), coral call-to-action (#F26B4E), with mustard, lilac, and dusty-blue accents. Rounded cards, soft shadows, paper texture. Avoid neon, tech-blue gradients, and glossy "AI" aesthetics.
- **Setting:** real life between languages — trains, kitchens, cafés, offices, a phone in one hand. The product is always shown inside a Telegram chat.
- **Music:** light, acoustic or lo-fi, unhurried.
